About Ascend

What does Ascend do?

Ascend is a student-focused AI career platform designed to help students navigate internships and entry-level job opportunities in a smarter, more personalized way.

Through its AI chatbot, Mira, Ascend understands a student’s academic background, skills, interests, work preferences, and career goals. Based on this profile, it recommends suitable career paths, required skills, skill gaps, and direct links to live job listings.

Instead of feeling like a generic career quiz, Ascend works like a guided conversation with a career mentor.

How Mira Helps

  • Personalizes recommendations based on student profiles.
  • Suggests internships and jobs aligned with skills and interests.
  • Allows multiple skills, interests, and custom inputs.
  • Provides required skills, skill gaps, and live job links.

About Ascend

What does Ascend do?

Ascend is a student-focused AI career platform designed to help students navigate internships and entry-level job opportunities in a smarter, more personalized way.

Through its AI chatbot, Mira, Ascend engages students in a natural conversation to understand their academic background, skills, interests, preferred work styles, and career goals. Based on this information, the platform recommends suitable career paths and provides direct access to live job opportunities from trusted platforms.

Rather than acting like a traditional career quiz, Ascend creates an interactive guidance experience that feels more like speaking to a career mentor.

How Mira Helps

  • Personalizes recommendations based on student profiles.
  • Suggests internships and jobs aligned with skills and interests.
  • Allows multiple skills, interests, and custom inputs.
  • Provides direct links to live job opportunities.
About Ascend

Helping students discover the right opportunities.

Ascend is an AI-powered student career assistant designed to help students identify internships and entry-level job opportunities that align with their background, skills, interests, and career goals.

Instead of relying on generic career quizzes, Ascend creates a conversational chatbot experience that asks personalized questions and recommends suitable opportunities with live job links.

Why Ascend?

  • Students often feel overwhelmed by too many career options.
  • Traditional job portals rarely personalize recommendations.
  • Many students are unsure which internships fit their profile.
  • Ascend bridges this gap through guided career matching.
